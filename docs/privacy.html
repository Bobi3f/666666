Русский

FIRST GEAR — игра, которую делает один человек. Игра не собирает и не передаёт ваши личные данные.

  • Сохранения и настройки (прогресс, деньги в игре, язык, имя и фамилия из профиля игрока) хранятся только на вашем устройстве — в браузере или в файлах игры. Они никуда не отправляются.

English

FIRST GEAR is a game made by one person. The game does not collect or share your personal data.

  • Saves and settings (progress, in-game money, language, the first and last name from the player profile) are stored only on your device — in the browser or in the game's files. They are never sent anywhere.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • "Report a bug" opens a GitHub page in your browser with a device summary (system, screen size, in-game day). Nothing is sent until you press "submit" on GitHub yourself.
  • Pi Network payments work only in Pi Browser through Pi's own system. Our server receives only the payment ID to credit the coins.
  • "Support the author" links (itch.io, Monobank) open in your browser; those sites' own rules apply there.
  • The game does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, contacts or location.
